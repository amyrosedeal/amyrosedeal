Students

Who I advise

I work with students in theoretical syntax, semantics, pragmatics, and morphology, generally on research projects involving under-described languages. My advisees have presented their work at top conferences in syntax and semantics and have competed successfully for the NSF graduate research fellowship. Former advisees are working in a variety of careers, including as professors, in university administration, and in the tech industry.

I encourage prospective graduate students interested in formal linguistics to contact me and to learn more about the Berkeley graduate program here.
